Outliers are the data points/extreme values which are very far from the mean and these data points cause unnecessary disturbance(asymmetric shape) to the distribution of data. The measure this asymmetry is called as Skewness. 


Types of Skewness: 

Positive Skewness / Right Tail / Right Skewed Data 

Negative Skewness / Left Tail / Left Skewed Data

If variance is 0 it indicates there are no patterns/variability in the data for the model to learn. Variance talks about individual variable information.

Correlation: It tells us both the Direction of the Relationship and also the strength of the relationship between 2 variables. 

Salary & Age are Positively related so correlation value will be a positive value.


Age(40) & Strength are Negatively related so correlation value will be a Negative Value. 


the range of correlation is between -1 to +1. 


if the correlation values is close to -1 : Strong Negative Relation B/w X & Y.
if the correlation values is close to -0.5 :Moderate Negative Relation B/w X & Y.
if the correlation values is close to 0 : NO Relation B/w X & Y.

if the correlation values is close to +0.5 : Moderate Positiv Relation B/w X & Y.

if the correlation values is close to +1 : Strong Positive Relation B/w X & Y.
